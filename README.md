## About
Reliant Solutions installs and services flat panel TVs, air conditioners,
washing machines, refrigerators and other appliances for domestic and
commercial customers nationwide, with offices in Karachi, Lahore and
Rawalpindi. This website presents the company's services and makes it easy
for customers to get in touch.
